# Primary 4 English Quiz: Subject–Verb Agreement

**Name:** ____________________  **Class:** __________  **Date:** __________

**Total: 20 marks**

## A. Choose the correct verb. (10 marks)

Circle the correct answer. Each question carries 1 mark.

1. My sister **(walk / walks)** to school every morning.
2. The boys **(play / plays)** football during recess.
3. Each pupil **(has / have)** a storybook.
4. The basket of apples **(is / are)** on the table.
5. Tom and Jerry **(enjoy / enjoys)** reading comics.
6. One of the dogs **(bark / barks)** loudly at strangers.
7. There **(is / are)** three pencils in the box.
8. Everyone **(want / wants)** to join the game.
9. You **(was / were)** very helpful yesterday.
10. The children **(do / does)** their homework after dinner.

## B. Fill in each blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets. (5 marks)

Use the **simple present tense**. Each question carries 1 mark.

11. Mrs Tan __________ us English every Monday. *(teach)*
12. The birds __________ over the playground every morning. *(fly)*
13. My brother __________ not like spicy food. *(do)*
14. Both girls __________ ready for the competition. *(be)*
15. The girl with two ribbons __________ beautifully. *(sing)*
